How it works

  1. Connect MetaMask wallet (import a Hardhat test account).
  2. Register by paying exactly 100,000 wei.
  3. Register resources: Water, Clothing, Medicine, Food.
  4. Update each resource quantity within 1 day to stay compliant.
  5. Non-compliant suppliers get deactivated; pay penalty to reactivate.
